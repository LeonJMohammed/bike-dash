# Changelog

## Unreleased
- Laps (2026-11-12, feature 5 of Leon's brief). Auto lap every 5 km to start (Settings > Ride > Auto lap: off, 1, 2, 5, 10 or 20 km, counted from the last lap), and a LAP button between PAUSE and HOLD TO STOP while riding; the History button now waits until the ride is over to make room. When a lap ends, its distance, moving time, average speed, average heart rate and average cadence (while pedalling) replace the ride numbers for 5 seconds, and a dimmed screen brightens; a tap goes back sooner. Moving time only. Every lap, the last stretch included, is saved with the ride (older rides still load); the summary and History list the splits, and History rows say how many laps.
- Eat / Drink reminders (2026-11-12, feature 4 of Leon's brief). Settings > Eating and drinking: a switch (off to start) and an interval from 15 to 60 minutes in 5-minute steps (30 to start). Counts moving time only, so it waits while the ride is paused. When due, a large orange "EAT / DRINK" banner across the top of the ride screen, a short buzz and the tri-tone chime, and a dimmed screen brightens (staying bright while the banner is up). Tap to dismiss, or it clears after 15 seconds, and the countdown restarts from then. The footer shows the minutes to the next one during a ride.
- Heart rate zone strip (2026-11-12, feature 3 of Leon's brief). A 7-point strip along the very bottom of the ride screen with five zones, grey, blue, green, orange and red, at 50–60, 60–70, 70–80, 80–90 and 90–100% of the max heart rate (new in Settings > Heart rate, 190 to start; the footer lists where each zone starts). The current zone is lit, the rest dimmed, with a white marker where the heart rate sits inside it; no numbers. It's hidden without a heart rate. The lit zone only changes once the heart rate is 2 bpm past a boundary, so it doesn't flicker, and a zone change brightens a dimmed screen.
- Screen dimming (2026-11-12, feature 2 of Leon's brief). During a ride, after 10 seconds without a touch, the screen dims to the level in Settings > Display (on by default, 20%, 5 to 60%; the slider previews the level). Any touch anywhere brightens it, and it dims again 10 seconds later. Normal brightness comes back when the ride ends, or when Control Center, a call or another app covers Bike Dash. Laps, eating reminders and heart rate zone changes will wake it too once those features exist. The screen still never locks during a ride.
- Re-sign job retries and says why (2026-11-12, Leon's OK). The first automatic renewal failed: overnight the iPhone was locked, on Wednesday it was away, and Xcode turned out to be signed out of the Apple ID. Now the MacBook starts 3 days before expiry instead of 2, tries every 15 minutes while due instead of every 3 hours, checks Xcode's sign-in and the phone (reachable, unlocked) before building, and the alert names the fix: unlock the iPhone, be home on Wi-Fi, or sign in to Xcode. Alerts are at most every 8 hours per kind and only 7 am to 10 pm unless it runs out within 6 hours. After Leon signed Xcode back in, a build fetched a fresh profile: signed until Thu 19 Nov 04:54 (build 27, with the OLED theme). Xcode 27 lists a signed-in account by identifier rather than email, which the sign-in check now recognises (d054784).
- OLED ride theme (2026-11-12, feature 1 of Leon's brief; not yet built: the MacBook was offline). Settings → Display → Ride theme: OLED black (default) or Standard. OLED black keeps the ride screen pure black: the ride, History and Settings buttons, the Spotify buttons and the GPS badge become thin outlines, and the music bar is black with a hairline on top. "Album colour behind the music" brings the album-coloured bar back. Colour stays on the numbers, the status dots and alerts (GPS FALLBACK). The ride screen has no continuous animations, so iOS keeps the display at its lowest refresh rate; no display link was added (see README).
- Speed and cadence sensors (2026-11-09, Leon's brief; not yet tested on a ride). Speed, distance and cadence now come from two Magene S3+ over Bluetooth, so GPS stays off to save battery. GPS takes over by itself if the speed sensor is disconnected for 10 s during a ride (GPS FALLBACK at the top), and hands back 5 s after it reconnects. With the sensor, auto-pause pauses as soon as the wheel stops (no turn for 3 s), counted from its last turn, and resumes when it turns. New on screen: a status strip (HR · SPEED · CADENCE dots) and a cadence tile; the grid is now Distance | Time, Speed, Cadence | Heart rate, Ascent | Avg pace. Settings has Speed from (Sensor or GPS), the wheel circumference (2105 mm by default) and two new screens: Pair sensors, and Sensor data with the raw values. 41 new unit tests, 88 in all.
- Re-sign watch on the PC (2026-11-07, Leon's request). The MacBook can't re-sign or send anything while it's asleep or off, so the always-on PC checks every 10 minutes and keeps its own copy of the expiry, read from the MacBook whenever it can reach it. When a re-sign is due and it can't reach the MacBook, ntfy says "Bike Dash: open the MacBook". That's only between 7 am and 10 pm, at most every 8 hours, high priority in the last 12 hours, and daily after expiry.
