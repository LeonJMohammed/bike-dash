# Changelog

## Unreleased
- Re-sign job retries and says why (2026-11-12, Leon's OK). The first automatic renewal failed: overnight the iPhone was locked, on Wednesday it was away, and Xcode turned out to be signed out of the Apple ID. Now the MacBook starts 3 days before expiry instead of 2, tries every 15 minutes while due instead of every 3 hours, checks Xcode's sign-in and the phone (reachable, unlocked) before building, and the alert names the fix: unlock the iPhone, be home on Wi-Fi, or sign in to Xcode. Alerts are at most every 8 hours per kind and only 7 am to 10 pm unless it runs out within 6 hours.
- OLED ride theme (2026-11-12, feature 1 of Leon's brief; not yet built: the MacBook was offline). Settings → Display → Ride theme: OLED black (default) or Standard. OLED black keeps the ride screen pure black: the ride, History and Settings buttons, the Spotify buttons and the GPS badge become thin outlines, and the music bar is black with a hairline on top. "Album colour behind the music" brings the album-coloured bar back. Colour stays on the numbers, the status dots and alerts (GPS FALLBACK). The ride screen has no continuous animations, so iOS keeps the display at its lowest refresh rate; no display link was added (see README).
- Speed and cadence sensors (2026-11-09, Leon's brief; not yet tested on a ride). Speed, distance and cadence now come from two Magene S3+ over Bluetooth, so GPS stays off to save battery. GPS takes over by itself if the speed sensor is disconnected for 10 s during a ride (GPS FALLBACK at the top), and hands back 5 s after it reconnects. With the sensor, auto-pause pauses as soon as the wheel stops (no turn for 3 s), counted from its last turn, and resumes when it turns. New on screen: a status strip (HR · SPEED · CADENCE dots) and a cadence tile; the grid is now Distance | Time, Speed, Cadence | Heart rate, Ascent | Avg pace. Settings has Speed from (Sensor or GPS), the wheel circumference (2105 mm by default) and two new screens: Pair sensors, and Sensor data with the raw values. 41 new unit tests, 88 in all.
- Re-sign watch on the PC (2026-11-07, Leon's request). The MacBook can't re-sign or send anything while it's asleep or off, so the always-on PC checks every 10 minutes and keeps its own copy of the expiry, read from the MacBook whenever it can reach it. When a re-sign is due and it can't reach the MacBook, ntfy says "Bike Dash: open the MacBook". That's only between 7 am and 10 pm, at most every 8 hours, high priority in the last 12 hours, and daily after expiry.
- First ride (2026-11-07): it went well. The Garmin comparison waits for the next ride, because the watch was started late.
- Re-sign job (2026-11-05): the MacBook renews the free 7-day signature when under 2 days are left, checking at login and every 3 hours. It sets Xcode's cached profile aside so Apple issues a fresh one, then rebuilds and reinstalls over Wi-Fi. Alerts go through ntfy, and Healthchecks.io gets a heartbeat. Tested the same day: a forced renewal got a new profile valid 7 days from then (Thu 12 Nov 20:41 became 22:14), plus a test alert and dry runs of every branch.
